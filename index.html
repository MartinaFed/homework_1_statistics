🍁 Analytical Observations

Observing the computation above reveals the unique geometric behaviors that make elliptic curve cryptography secure.

The Avalanche Effect

Notice how adding a point to itself over a finite field causes extreme, unpredictable jumps across the canvas.

The jump from step k to k+1 has no obvious geometric relationship. This deterministic scattering guarantees that finding the multiplier k from the final point is mathematically infeasible.

Hashing vs. Curves

Hash Functions: Act like a digital shredder. They destroy the input's structure completely to create a one-way, fixed-size output.

Elliptic Curves: Act like an intricate, invisible maze. The output looks random, but secretly retains an elegant algebraic symmetry.
